Research

How can AI systems learn reliable representations of complex data when supervision is limited, prior knowledge is available, and the structures of interest are small, sparse, or difficult to annotate?

These are general machine learning problems: learning from limited and noisy supervision, combining complementary inductive biases, transferring across data sources, and building efficient models. Biomedical imaging is the demanding testbed where I study them; the methods are designed to extend to other volumetric, multi-channel, and multimodal data in science and engineering.

Selected Projects

Published · AIPR 2025

HE3D-Net: 3D Ensembles & Hybrid Models for Neonatal Brain Lesion Segmentation

A heterogeneous ensemble of 3D transformer-based and convolutional models for Hypoxic-Ischemic Encephalopathy (HIE) diagnosis and lesion segmentation from multi-channel diffusion MRI (BONBID-HIE 2024 Grand Challenge data), where lesions range from a few voxels to large regions.

  • A post-competition submission (thresholded ensemble output, multi-channel input) ranks 1st overall (mean position 1.7) on the public test-phase leaderboard: Dice 0.670 (rank 1), NSD 0.774 (rank 1), MASD 2.685 (rank 3). View leaderboard ↗
  • Developed 3D SDENet and a 2D+3D fusion in which classical image-processing features complement learned representations, yielding a simpler model with lower computational cost.
  • Zero-shot evaluation of medical foundation models (MedSAM2, SAM-Med3D) showed they did not generalize well to this data.
  • In progress: extending SDENet to multimodal learning combining 3D MRI with clinical variables (field strength, age at scan, hypothermia, seizures).
  • 3D Segmentation
  • Ensembles
  • Diffusion MRI
  • Hybrid Models
Dissertation · In progress

Joint 3D Blood & Lymphatic Vessel Segmentation from Multi-Channel Microscopy

Self-supervised, supervised, and weakly supervised methods for simultaneous segmentation of thin, branching blood and lymphatic vessels in 3D fluorescence microscopy of mouse dura mater, a relatively uncommon dataset containing both vessel types.

  • Ensemble learning–based silver truth generation that reduces manual correction time by nearly 50% compared with the previous active-learning workflow.
  • Evaluated foundation models SAM-Med3D and VesselFM on this dataset.
  • Investigating Swin-UNETR and ensemble architectures for topologically complex, sparsely annotated volumes, with planned extension to other tubular structures.
  • Paper in preparation: Colocalized 3D Blood and Lymphatic Microvasculature Networks Using Vision Transformers.
  • Self-Supervised Learning

  • Microscopy
  • Label Efficiency

Spatial Spread of Influenza A Virus in Commercial Swine Farms

With biology collaborators, analyzed genomic data on disease transmission and evolution, including Bayesian phylogeographic analysis of Influenza A virus sequences across compartments in U.S. swine farms (first author, Emerging Microbes & Infections, 2024).

Future Directions

Learning algorithms that combine self-supervised representations, limited or noisy labels, and prior knowledge such as intensity, texture, spatial relationships, and connectivity.

  • When does each source of information improve generalization, and when does it introduce bias?
  • How can ensemble-based silver truth and pseudo-labeling reduce annotation cost without propagating errors on small structures?
  • Do priors work best as inputs, architectural components, or training constraints?

Joint representations from images, non-imaging variables, and molecular data, and parameter-efficient adaptation of foundation models to 3D, multi-channel, and multimodal inputs.

  • Modality imbalance: a high-dimensional image dominating a few informative variables.
  • Missing modalities, which are common in real data, and cross-modal alignment.
  • Longer term: vision-language models linking images with reports and metadata, with generalization tested on held-out data sources (e.g., TCIA, TCGA).

Uncertainty-aware prediction, distribution-shift detection, and evaluation reported across object size, data source, and acquisition conditions, so users know when a prediction should be trusted.

  • When can compact, hybrid models match larger ones at lower computational cost?
  • Deployment in resource-constrained settings.
  • Evaluating the reliability of generative models.
